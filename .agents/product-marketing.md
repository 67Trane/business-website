# Product marketing context: mehmet-deliaci.net

Read by the marketing skills (copywriting, cro, marketing-psychology, …) before any copy or
conversion work. Facts only; anything not listed here needs the owner's confirmation first.

## Offer

- Mehmet Deliaci, one-person web design and development business in Weißenburg in Bayern.
- Sells custom websites with animation for local businesses, designed phone-first.
  No templates or site builders; colours and fonts come from the client's logo and premises.
- Price: compact company website from 1.000 €, fixed-price quote after a free intro call.
- Timeline: compact site usually online in 2 to 4 weeks (owner's estimate).
- Reply to enquiries within 24 hours. Works on site in the region and remote.
- Languages: German, English, Turkish (useful for Turkish-owned businesses).
- Aftercare on request: updates, security, small changes sent via WhatsApp.

## Audience

- Owners of small local businesses: restaurants and Imbiss, barbers and hairdressers,
  car and tyre workshops, estate agents, trades. Often not technical, short on time.
- ~90 % of visitors arrive on a smartphone from Google Ads. Mobile is the primary view.
- Their worries: cost, getting a template that looks like everyone else's, agencies that
  are hard to reach, having to write texts themselves.

## Primary conversion

Free intro call (contact form, phone, WhatsApp). Secondary: open an industry demo.

## Proof that exists (usable)

- Four live industry demos at mehmet-deliaci.net/demos/: bosporus (Gastronomie), immobilienmakler
  (Weber & Stein, fictional), jefferson (Barbershop), reifenservice (Kfz). Never mention "herrmann".
- This site itself (performance and accessibility measured with Lighthouse).
- Portrait and direct phone number: the person is the brand.

## Claims that are NOT supported yet (do not write; unlock in brackets)

- Results like "more calls / more customers / +X % enquiries" [client numbers after launch].
- Testimonials or reviews [real client quotes with permission].
- Client logos, "X projects delivered" [actual delivered client projects].
- A free design preview before payment [owner confirms it is part of the offer].

## Voice

Sie-Form, plain German, first person ("ich"). Concrete details over adjectives. No AI tells
(no "nicht X, sondern Y", no fragment rows, no forced threes, no dashes as connectors).
Copy lives in `src/app/data/i18n.data.ts` (de/en/tr).

## Learnings

- 2026-11-23: Hero headline "Websites, die Eindruck machen." rejected by the owner as very weak
  (vague, no reader, fails the swap test). Next try "Eine Website, so gut wie Ihre Arbeit." also
  dropped: fails the swap test, reads as performed cleverness.
- 2026-11-23: Owner chose "So kann Ihre neue Website aussehen." (demo as proof, works together
  with the three demo phones in the hero). Untested runner-ups for an A/B test:
  "Ihr Laden sieht besser aus als Ihre Website?" (pain, contrast),
  "Ihr Webdesigner aus Weißenburg, direkt am Telefon." (only one that fully passes the swap test),
  "Ihre Kunden suchen Sie auf dem Handy. Was finden sie dort?" (self-check, long on mobile).
- 2026-11-23: Owner found most copy "AI slop"; rewritten with the humanizer rules.
